| D36 | 23 Nov | Judge (K2): tie-break replica only on category disagreement (`max_replicas` 3), invalid answers replaced; replicas never see each other's verdicts; repeat sample 10 % by item hash, measurement only; windows over 1,200 tokens with one shared sentence and a 15 % source margin; `max_windows` 8 as a cost guard (new default); bake-off compares at default thresholds and stops on max(reported, list-price) spend | Majority comes from fusion's noisy-OR (two of three replicas flag, one reaches only the review queue); a replica that saw another's answer would not be an independent vote |
| D37 | 23 Nov | ASR chunking: primary transcription cut at a sentence pause near every 10 s (6-14 s), else the nearest sentence pause up to 29 s, else a word gap near 10 s, else the quietest point at 18-29 s; second opinion on long chunks (18-29 s) at least 1.5 s from every primary cut, one chunk for 14-29 s audio, a middle split for short audio.
